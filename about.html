Aziz Uthman Computers & Requisites Trading Co. L.L.C. supplies computer hardware, components and accessories from our shop in Bur Dubai. We serve walk-in customers, home builders, small offices and trade buyers who need parts in hand quickly rather than a two-week import wait.

The business is built on a simple idea: if it is listed, it is on the shelf. Stock levels on this site reflect what is physically in the shop, so when you order, you are ordering something we can put in a driver's hands the same day.

What we do

  • Retail and trade supply of components, laptops, monitors and peripherals
  • Custom PC builds assembled, cable-managed and stress-tested in-house
  • Upgrades, diagnostics and warranty handling for machines bought from us
  • Bulk quotations with tax invoice for offices and institutions across the UAE
